# Clinical Background & Workflow

The **Skin Screening Implementation Guide** supports standardized screening, documentation, and referral workflows for early detection of cutaneous malignancies, including melanoma, basal cell carcinoma (BCC), and squamous cell carcinoma (SCC).

## Clinical Screening Pathway

1. **Patient Risk Assessment:** Assessment of individual risk factors including Fitzpatrick Skin Phototype (I–VI), UV exposure history, and family/personal history of skin cancer.
2. **Clinical & Dermoscopic Examination:** Visual and dermoscopic examination of suspicious skin lesions using structured morphological criteria (such as the ABCDE criteria).
3. **Documentation:** Capturing the finding using the `SkinLesionObservation` and `FitzpatrickSkinTypeObservation` profiles.
4. **Triage & Referral:** Escalation to teledermatology assessment, surgical biopsy, or ongoing longitudinal lesion monitoring.

## Terminologies Used
* **SNOMED CT:** Standardized clinical finding concepts and anatomical body site locations.
* **LOINC:** Standardized observation identifiers and clinical scale answer sets.
