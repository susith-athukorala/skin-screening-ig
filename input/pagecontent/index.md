# Skin Screening FHIR Implementation Guide

Welcome to the **Skin Screening Implementation Guide**, developed at the **Rosemary Bryant AO Research Centre (RBRC)**, Adelaide University.

## Purpose & Clinical Scope
This implementation guide standardizes data exchange for:
* **Patient Skin Cancer Risk Profiles:** Capturing phototypes (Fitzpatrick scale), family history, and UV exposure.
* **Lesion-Level Examination Findings:** Standardized anatomical mapping, morphological characteristics (ABCDE criteria), and dermoscopic features.
* **Teledermatology & Clinical Workflow:** Asynchronous primary-to-specialist referrals, biopsy requests, and monitoring logs.
